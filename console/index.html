    Conversion

    signed capacity by issuer, GW

    Quarter-over-quarter change

    what the brief reports: the step each quarter, not the level · bars share one GW scale

    Dominion contracted-capacity ladder

    the one series published on a consistent basis across all three stages

    Dominion CLOA → ESA conversion

    ESA added in the quarter as a share of the prior quarter's CLOA book

    Definitions are not standardised across companies. "Signed ESA", "committed", "contracted" and "advanced stage" mean different things in each deck, and pipeline denominators differ in both stage and horizon. Read across issuers as indicative of relative conversion discipline, never like-for-like. Exelon changed methodology at Q2 2026 and is not comparable to its own earlier figures. Entergy never states an absolute signed figure and is therefore absent. Every point is drawn live from the metrics table — nothing here is hand-maintained.
