<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Video of Board of Supervisors&#8217; Meetings Online</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Henrico is pleased to offer live web-streaming of the regular meetings of the Board of Supervisors. You can <a href="/supervisors/video-of-meetings/">view the meetings</a>, or the meeting archives &#8211; held on the second and fourth Tuesday of each month. In addition to watching live webcasts of meetings, you can also see past meetings. Videos will be stored in an online archive, which will allow viewers to search individual agenda items from a particular meeting or watch a meeting in its entirety.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Board of Supervisors&#8217; Meeting Summaries and Minutes</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The County Manager&#8217;s Office publishes the Agend-O-Gram, which is a summary of actions taken at regular meetings of the Board of Supervisors. Select a date to see the status of items considered by the Board. You can also <a href="/manager/supervisors-agenda-o-gram/subscribe/">subscribe to receive e-mail notices</a> when Agend-O-Grams have been published online. Formal minutes of regular and special meetings of the Board of Supervisors are posted in PDF format subsequent to approval by the Board. Please note that these are large files.</p>
